# Layers and objects panel: a tree of layers and objects with lock, eye, opacity, rename and drag

Status: Draft (the criteria are complete and testable; becomes Ready when `adrs.md` is confirmed by the architect, the customer has accepted the amendment of ADR 0002 §5 it proposes, and the UX notes are confirmed by the ux-engineer, `CLAUDE.md` §4; it is built after `0023-groups`)
Priority: Must (it delivers the layers half of R-EDIT-009, which is in the customer-confirmed MVP, and replaces the placeholder `0039-layers`; the customer's panel wishes are the Origin)
Origin: Customer (request of 2026-10-10: an overview very similar to Inkscape's Layers and Objects dialog, a tree of layers, objects, groups, compound paths and paths, with lock, visibility and opacity per layer and per object, select by clicking a row in both directions, rename, reorder by drag, drag into and out of layers, new and delete layer, collapse and expand). The placement in the Properties panel, the decisions in "Decided by the product owner" and every criterion marked **Proposal** are my own and not accepted until the customer says so.

## User value

As a maker I want one list that shows everything in my drawing as layers, groups and objects, so that I can name the layers for "cut", "engrave" and "reference", hide or lock the ones I am not working on, fade one, find an object I cannot click, and change what lies on top of what, without hunting on the canvas.

**What the other tools do, and what we do.**

- *Inkscape* 1.2 and later: one Layers and Objects dialog, a tree with a row per layer, group and object, an eye and a lock on each row (a Shift+click applies the change to other items), a layer-only view, drag to reorder, rename by double-click, and a hover over a row highlights the object on the canvas. Layers are groups with a flag. The dialog is a dock that takes canvas width, and the object lists of older Inkscape versions are known to be slow with thousands of objects. The details of Inkscape's dialog should be checked against the version the customer uses.
- *Illustrator*: a Layers panel with sublayers, an eye, a lock, a target circle and a colour per layer. *Affinity*: a Layers panel with an opacity slider and blend mode above the list, and a lock and visibility toggle per row.
- *LightBurn*: layers are a colour palette with separate Show and Output toggles per layer, not a tree. It has no object list.

**What we do the same:** the tree, an eye and a lock on every row, rename, drag to reorder, the opacity of the selected row above the list (Affinity), layers as the unit that cut and engrave roles will use later (`0027`).
**What we do differently:**

- *It lives in the Properties panel as a tab* (decision 1), so the canvas keeps its 520 px at 800 x 600. Inkscape's dock takes canvas width.
- *Opacity is a value field above the list, not a popup.* Every row also shows its own opacity when it is not 100 %.
- *It scales.* The list is virtualised; 10,000 objects draw about 60 rows (criteria 48 to 52). A hidden layer is one write, not one per object.
- *A hidden or locked object is never selected,* by any route (criterion 14). The selection is always something you can see and edit.
- *Everything that happens here is a step you can take back* once `0020` exists (criterion 44), the eye and the lock included.

## Words used below

- **Node:** anything in the document tree: a leaf (path, compound path, rectangle, ellipse, polygon, star), a clone (`0049`), a group (`0023`) or a layer.
- **Layer:** a node that is a group (`0023`) with a layer marker. It lives at the top level of the tree only (decision 2). It is a container, not an object: it is never part of the selection (decision 3).
- **Loose object:** a node at the top level of the tree that is not in a layer. A document may have layers, loose objects, or both (decision 2).
- **Row:** one line of the list for one node. The list shows nodes depth first, the front-most at the top.
- **Own flags:** the `visible`, `locked` and `opacity` registers of a node. **Effective** hidden or locked: the node's own flag or that of any ancestor. Effective opacity: criterion 27.
- **Panel target:** what the Opacity field and the footer act on: the selected nodes, or, when the maker last clicked a layer row, that layer (criterion 13).
- **Active layer:** the layer that new objects are added to (criterion 35). View state, not saved.
- **Context:** the document top level or the entered group of `0023`. **Top-level object of the context:** a direct child of the entered group, or, at the document top level, a loose object or a direct child of a layer (layers are transparent to selection, criterion 42).
- Lengths and angles as in the earlier specs.

## Acceptance criteria

### The tab

1. Given the Properties panel, then its strip has the tabs Document, Style, **Layers**, History in this order (History exists once `0020` has merged; until then three tabs). The Layers tab has a Lucide `Layers` icon, a hit area of 36 x 28 px, the accessible name "Layers" and a text-only tooltip after 400 ms and on keyboard focus: "Layers (Shift+Ctrl+L)". It is never dimmed: it is available with an empty document. The strip stays fixed in size and order (`0043` criteria 1, 19).
2. Given the Layers tab is active, then the panel never switches by itself, whatever the selection does (the rule of History, `0043` criterion 8), and a selection change never makes Layers active. Given **Shift+Ctrl+L** (Shift+Cmd+L on macOS), then the panel expands if it is collapsed, Layers becomes active and focus moves to the list; the first selected row is scrolled into view. It is ignored during a canvas drag. Given **Shift+Ctrl+F** with Layers active, then Style or Document becomes active as `0043` criterion 14 says for History.
3. Given the Layers tab, then the body is a column that fills the panel height: a header row with the **Opacity** value field (criterion 26), the list (the one scroll area of the tab, minimum height 120 px), and a footer with the buttons **New layer** and **Delete layer** (criteria 34, 36). The strip, the header row and the footer do not scroll. The panel stays 280 px wide with a content width of 244 px, and the canvas region does not change width when the tab is shown (520 px at an 800 x 600 window). At 800 x 600 the list shows at least 12 rows.
4. Given the Layers tab, then the subject line reads "40 objects" (all nodes that are not layers; "1 object" for one), with an ellipsis and the full text in a tooltip when it does not fit. With four tabs the subject line has 80 px (four tabs of 36 px and three gaps of 4 px are 156 px, and 8 px separate them from the subject line, as in `0043`; with three tabs it has 120 px). It is cut with an ellipsis and is never the only place that states a fact.

### The tree

5. Given the document, then the list has one row for every node, depth first, with the front-most node at the top: the first row is the last child at the top level, and inside a container the last child is its first row. Collapsed containers hide their descendants. A loose object and a layer are rows of the same level, ordered by their stacking position. Test: three loose rectangles A, B, C (C on top) give the rows C, B, A; a layer between B and C gives C, the layer (with its children), B, A.
6. Given a row, then it is 28 px high and has, from the left: an indent of 16 px per level (at most 6 levels, 96 px; deeper rows keep that indent, and `aria-level` carries the true depth), a chevron for containers, a kind glyph of 16 px (layer `Layers`, group `Group`, path `Spline`, compound path, rectangle `Square`, ellipse `Circle`, polygon, star, clone `Link2`; the ux-engineer chooses the rest), the name, the node's opacity as a muted number such as "60%" only when it is not 100 %, an eye button and a lock button of 24 px each, and, on hover, on focus within, on the selected rows and on the active layer row, a row-action slot (criterion 13), drawn over the end of the name so that nothing reflows when it appears. Glyphs are shapes, never colour alone.
7. Given a node with no name, then its row shows "<Kind> <six-character id>" (for example "Rectangle 3f9a1c", the form the History tab uses, `0041`), a clone "Clone of <origin's name>", a group "Group <id>". A layer always has a name: "Layer 1" when created (the smallest positive N with no layer named "Layer N"). A long name is cut with an ellipsis; the full name is in the tooltip.
8. Given a container row, then the chevron or the Right and Left keys expand and collapse it. At open, layers are expanded and groups collapsed; the state is kept per node while the node exists, is not saved, is no step and is dropped by New and Open. A collapsed container that has a selected descendant shows a 6 px mark after its name (shape, with the tooltip "Contains the selection").
9. Given a document with no node, then the list shows "No objects yet." and a muted line "Draw something or add a layer."; the footer is still there.

### Selecting by row, and the canvas

10. Given a click on the row of a leaf, clone or group, then exactly that node is selected (it replaces the selection) and the canvas shows its box and handles in the same frame. Given its parent is a group other than the current context, then that group is entered first and the node is selected in it (`0023` criterion 15, dimming and indicator included); given its parent is a layer or the document top level, the context is the top level. The Layers tab stays active.
11. Given Ctrl+click on a row, then the node is added to or removed from the selection; given Shift+click, then the selection becomes the range of rows between the anchor row (the last clicked) and the clicked row that share the clicked row's parent. A Ctrl or Shift click on a row with another parent than the current selection's context acts as a plain click, because a selection lives in one context (`0023`). A marquee, lasso or canvas click works as before.
12. Given any change of the canvas selection (click, marquee, lasso, Ctrl+A, undo, a peer's delete), then the rows of the selected nodes show the selected look (`aria-selected`) in the same frame, the collapsed ancestors of the first selected row are expanded (view state), further selected rows inside collapsed containers show the mark of criterion 8, and the first selected row is scrolled into view unless the change came from a click in this list (Ctrl+A over 10,000 objects must not open every layer).
13. Given a click on a layer row, then the layer becomes the active layer and the panel target, the canvas selection is cleared, and no node is selected (layers are not selectable objects, decision 3). A layer row has the row action **Select contents** (an icon button of 24 px; a keyboard route via criterion 38), which selects the layer's top-level children that are not effectively hidden or locked, in their stacking order, at once. Ctrl+click and Shift+click on a layer row act as a plain click.
14. Given any state, then the selection never contains a node that is effectively hidden or effectively locked: such a row ignores a click (the row is focused, the selection is unchanged, the tooltip says "Hidden" or "Locked"), and a selected node that becomes effectively hidden or locked by any route, including a peer's change or a layer being hidden, leaves the selection in the same frame. Given the entered group becomes hidden or locked, then the context falls back to the nearest visible ancestor (`0023` criterion 20).
15. **Proposal.** Given the pointer or keyboard focus on the row of a leaf, clone or group, then the hover box of `0014` is drawn on that node (a group's box for a group), and it is removed in the frame the pointer or focus leaves. A layer row draws nothing.

### Rename

16. Given F2 on a focused row, or a double-click on a name, then the name becomes a text field with the current name selected. Enter or a press elsewhere commits it, Escape cancels. The result is one step (`rename_node`). At most 128 characters are accepted; leading and trailing white space is trimmed; control characters are dropped; an empty result removes the name, so the default name of criterion 7 shows again. Two nodes may have the same name. Rename is allowed on a locked node.
17. Given a name that holds markup or direction controls (`<img src=x onerror=alert(1)>`, a right-to-left override U+202E), then the row, the tooltip, the accessible name and the History tab show it as plain text, and bidirectional control characters (U+202A to U+202E, U+2066 to U+2069) are dropped when the name is committed and when it is read from a file. Given a name in a file longer than 128 characters, then the row shows its first 128 and the file is left as it is.

### Visibility, lock and opacity

18. Given a click on the eye of a row, then the node's own `visible` flag toggles, one step (`set_node_visible`); when the row is one of several selected rows, the same flag is set on all of them in that one step. The glyph is `Eye` or `EyeOff`, so state is a shape.
19. Given an effectively hidden node (own flag off, or any ancestor's), then: it is not drawn; it is not hit by a click, marquee, lasso, Alt-click cycle or Ctrl+A; the eyedropper (`0017`, `0040`) does not sample it (where nothing else is painted it picks the background); it is not a target of the Pen's continue and connect (`0034`) or the Node tool; it is not part of "Fit to content" (`0015`) or the status-bar measures; and it is not output (criterion 24). Test per item.
20. Given a click on the lock of a row, then the node's own `locked` flag toggles, one step (`set_node_locked`), multi-row as criterion 18. An effectively locked node is still drawn; it is not hit by a click, marquee, lasso, Alt-click cycle or Ctrl+A; it cannot be selected in the panel (criterion 14); it cannot be dragged to another place in the list or deleted from the list, and no node can be dropped into a locked container; its opacity cannot be changed; the eyedropper does sample it; a locked node is output. Hide, show, lock, unlock and rename always work.
21. Given a selected group or a "Select contents" selection, then a transform, style edit, Delete or reorder of the group acts on all its descendants, locked ones included: a lock protects a node from being picked and edited itself, not from being carried along by the container the maker is working on (decision 4, as in Figma).
22. Given a node under a hidden or locked ancestor, then its eye or lock shows the inherited state (the glyph in the muted, dashed form the ux-engineer defines, and the accessible description "Hidden by layer Cut"); a click toggles the node's own flag only; showing or unlocking the ancestor restores the node as it was, including its own flags.
23. Given the active layer is effectively locked or hidden, when the maker starts to draw with the Pen or a shape tool, then nothing is created and a notice appears in the canvas notice slot: "Layer Cut is locked. Unlock it or choose another layer." / "Layer Cut is hidden. Show it or choose another layer." (the object would be invisible or unchangeable). The tool stays.
24. Given the core's single output list (`Document::output_objects()` or the architect's equivalent: every exporter and the job generator read it), then it holds every effectively visible leaf in stacking order with its effective opacity (a clone is handed over as the path it shows, `0049`), and leaves out hidden ones; locked ones are in it. This is the decision "hidden objects are not exported or produced" (Question 2). `0024` and `0029` use the list and carry the end-to-end tests; this criterion pins it in the core.
25. Given an origin that is hidden or locked (`0049`), then its clones are unaffected: a clone has its own flags and is drawn from the origin's outline and style whether or not the origin is shown.
26. Given the Opacity field in the header, then it is the value field of `0017` (drag to scrub, click to type, integer percent 0 to 100, live while dragging, one commit and one step on release or Enter, Escape restores) with the label "Opacity" and a muted caption under it naming the panel target ("3 objects", "Layer Cut"). It sets the node `opacity` of every selected node, or of the active layer when a layer row was clicked last. With different values it shows "Mixed". It is not in the tree when the target is effectively locked (the caption reads "Locked") or when nothing is targeted. The step is `set_node_opacity`.
27. Given the rendering, then the alpha of each paint of a leaf is its own paint opacity (fill opacity, stroke opacity) times the leaf's `opacity` times the `opacity` of every ancestor group and layer; each paint is multiplied separately, nothing is composited as a group (Question 5). Test: fill opacity 80 %, leaf 50 %, layer 50 %: the fill is painted at 20 % alpha, and so is the stroke at its own 100 %, 25 %. The dimming of `0023` criterion 15 multiplies on top. Consequence, stated in the UX notes: where a fill and a stroke of one node overlap, or two nodes of one layer overlap, the lower shows through, which SVG's group opacity would not show.
28. Given a clone (`0049`), then its alpha is the origin's paint opacity times the origin's own `opacity` times the clone's own `opacity` times the clone's ancestors' (the origin's ancestors do not count).

### Reordering and moving

29. Given a press on a row's glyph or name and a drag past 4 px, then the row (or all selected rows when the pressed row is selected, in their relative order) follows the pointer; a 2 px insertion line shows where it would land (before or after a row) or the container row is outlined (inside it, as its front-most child); the list scrolls at its edges; Escape cancels and writes nothing; the release is one commit and one step (`move_nodes`). The new stacking order is the list order: after the drop the node lies where the line was.
30. Given a drop, then it is allowed for a leaf, clone or group among its siblings, at the top level, into a layer, or into a group (depth at most 32, `0023` criterion 5); for a layer only between top-level rows (a layer is never inside a group or another layer); never into the node itself or one of its descendants; never into a locked container; never of a locked node. Given a refused drop, then the cursor shows "not allowed", the tooltip says why ("Layers stay at the top level", "Locked", "Groups can be nested 32 levels deep"), and nothing changes.
31. Given a move to another parent, then each moved node keeps its id, geometry, style, flags, name and clone links (a clone and its origin stay linked), and a drop into a layer or group at another position changes only the stacking order, not how anything looks except by the new order and the new ancestors' flags and opacity.
32. Given a focused row and **Alt+Up** or **Alt+Down**, then the selected rows (or the focused row) move one place toward the front or the back among their siblings; **Alt+Home** and **Alt+End** move them to the front or the back; **Alt+Left** moves them out of their container to the place directly in front of it; **Alt+Right** moves them into the container row directly in front of them as its front-most child. Each is one step (`move_nodes`), refused by the rules of criterion 30, announced for assistive technology ("Moved Rectangle 3f9a1c to position 2 of 5"), and keeps the focus on the moved row. These are the keyboard alternative to dragging. The ux-engineer checks that WebKitGTK, WebView2, WKWebView and the browser build deliver Alt+Left and Alt+Right to the page.
33. **Proposal.** Given the Select tool, a selection and the canvas focused, then **Home** brings it to the front of its siblings, **End** sends it to the back, **Page Up** moves it one place toward the front and **Page Down** one toward the back, keeping the relative order of several selected nodes; one step (`move_nodes`), gated like every letter shortcut (`0010` criterion 55), `preventDefault` when handled. This delivers the "bring to front" commands that `0016` left out. The Select-bar tooltip gets no new line; the shortcut table gets four rows.

### Layers, the active layer and delete

34. Given **New layer**, then an empty layer named "Layer N" is created directly in front of the active layer (at the front of the top level when there is none), becomes the active layer and the panel target, its row is scrolled into view and its name is in edit (criterion 16), so the maker can type "Cut" at once; Escape keeps the default name. One step (`new_layer`; the rename inside it is part of the same step when committed at once). A layer cannot be created inside a group or another layer.
35. Given an active layer, then it is marked by a 3 px bar at the row's left edge and `aria-current="true"`; every tool that creates an object (Pen, Rectangle, Ellipse, Polygon/star, and the results of commands that create instead of replace) puts it at the front of the active layer, unless a group is entered, in which case it goes at the front of that group (`0023` criterion 19). With no layer in the document, objects go to the top level as today. Selecting an object makes its layer the active layer (a loose object makes none); at Open it is the front-most layer, if any. The active layer is view state: not saved, no step.
36. Given **Delete layer** (the footer button, shown only with an active layer) or the Delete key on a focused layer row, then an empty layer is deleted at once; a layer with content first shows, until `0020` has merged, an inline confirm in place of the footer, no popup: "Delete layer Cut and its 12 objects?" with the buttons "Delete layer" and "Cancel" (Escape cancels; the text adds "This cannot be undone yet."). Once `0020` has merged the layer is deleted at once, like any selection, because Ctrl+Z takes it back (the design system's rule: an action Ctrl+Z reverses never asks), and the notice reads "Deleted layer Cut and 12 objects." The build that merges second removes the confirm (`0020` criterion 52). The layer, its descendants and their rows go in one commit, one step (`delete_layer`); clones outside the layer whose origin was in it are unlinked in that commit (`0049` criterion 31). A locked layer refuses with the notice "Layer Cut is locked. Unlock it first." and nothing changes. Afterwards the active layer is the next one toward the back, or the next toward the front, or none.
37. Given Delete on a focused object row or on the canvas, then the selected nodes are deleted exactly as the canvas Delete does; an effectively locked node cannot be selected, so it cannot be deleted this way. Deleting or moving out the last child of a layer leaves the empty layer in place (unlike an empty group, which `0023` criterion 14 removes).

### Keyboard and accessibility

38. Given the list, then it is one Tab stop with roving focus: `role="treegrid"` named "Layers and objects", rows `role="row"` with `aria-level`, `aria-posinset`, `aria-setsize`, `aria-selected` and, for containers, `aria-expanded`; cells for the name (with the chevron), the eye, the lock and the row action (for a layer Select contents, for a clone Select original, `0049`; no cell for other rows). Up and Down move between rows, Home and End to the first and last, Page Up and Page Down by a page. In the name cell Right expands a collapsed container and otherwise moves to the next cell, Left collapses an expanded container and otherwise moves to the previous cell or, in the name cell of a child, to its parent row. Enter or Space on an eye, lock or Select contents cell presses it.
39. Given a focused row, then Enter or Space on the name cell selects it (criterion 10, for a layer criterion 13), Ctrl+Space toggles it, Shift+Up and Shift+Down extend the selection among siblings, F2 renames, Delete deletes (criteria 36, 37). The tool letters of the canvas (`0010` criterion 55) are ignored while the list has focus, as for any focused control.
40. Given a press on a row with the mouse, then focus stays in the list so the arrows work; Escape in the list returns focus to the canvas and never clears the selection (`0043` UX notes, Escape in the strip). A press on an eye or lock keeps the focus where it was.
41. Given a row, then its accessible name is "Rectangle 3f9a1c, rectangle, hidden, locked, opacity 60 percent" (hidden, locked and opacity only when they apply, an inherited state marked "by layer Cut"); state is never colour alone; glyphs have at least 3:1 against the panel and the focus ring is 2 px; targets are at least 24 x 24 px; nothing animates apart from the scroll at a drag edge, so `prefers-reduced-motion` needs nothing.

### The commands that read the tree

42. Given Ctrl+A (`0044`), marquee, lasso and the Alt-click cycle at the document top level, then layers are transparent: they choose among the loose objects and the direct children of all layers that are effectively visible and not locked, in stacking order across layers; Ctrl+A in an entered group chooses its direct children as before. A click on an object in a layer selects the object (or its top-level group), not the layer. This amends `0044` criterion 1 and `0023` criteria 8 to 10 (Question 6).
43. Given a command that acts on the selection (Booleans, Combine, Break apart, Split, Fracture, Flatten, Offset, Object to path, Group, a transform, a style edit, Linked clone), then it never sees a hidden or locked node, because the selection cannot hold one (criterion 14); a result takes the place of its base operand inside the same parent, layer or group included (`0016` criterion 22, `0023` criterion 19).

### History, the file and hostile input

44. Given `0020` has merged, then each of these is one step with an operation name from `0020`'s table: New layer, Delete layer, Rename, Hide or Show, Lock or Unlock, Opacity, and Move or Reorder (drag, keys, or the canvas keys of criterion 33). A toggle applied to several selected rows is one step. Expanding, collapsing, the active layer, the panel target and the scroll position are not steps. The guard of `0020` criterion 41 fails the build without these entries.
45. Given Ctrl+Z after Delete layer, then the layer returns with its id, its name and flags, its children in their order with their ids, and the clones that were unlinked are clones again (ADR 0014 §11 and the kind change of `0049` criterion 36). After a Hide, a Lock, an Opacity change or a Move, Ctrl+Z restores the previous value or place field by field.
46. Given a project with layers, groups, names, flags and opacities is saved and reopened, then the tree, the order, every name, every flag and every opacity are exactly as before; `format_version` goes to the next free number at merge (`specs/README.md`). A file with any of the new registers opened by an earlier build is refused with "saved by a newer version" (an earlier build would draw a hidden object and ignore a lock; for a hidden object that is output it would cut something the maker had taken out). A file from an earlier build opens unchanged: no layers, nothing hidden or locked, opacity 100 %, and it is not rewritten on open. A golden file `layers_v<N>.curvyo` pins the format.
47. Given a file in which `opacity` is not a number or lies outside 0 to 1, `visible` or `locked` is not a boolean, or `name` is not a string, then the file is refused as damaged and the editor does not crash. Given a layer marker on a node that is not at the top level, then it is read as an ordinary group. Given 10,000 layers, or a tree nested deeper than 32 levels (`0023` criterion 27), then the first opens without hang and the second is refused as damaged.

### Performance

Measured on the CI runner, release build, native; the browser build may take up to twice as long.

48. Given a project with 10,000 objects in 20 layers, then the Layers tab shows its first rows within 100 ms of the press on its tab, the list holds at most 60 row elements in the page at any time, the scrollbar is exact from the first frame, and scrolling from the top to the end in one jump draws the last rows within one frame (16 ms).
49. Given 10,000 objects, when the maker presses Ctrl+A on the canvas, then the canvas and the rows show the selection within 100 ms (`0044` criterion 5), and the panel stays responsive (the list scrolls during the next frame).
50. Given a layer of 10,000 objects, then hiding it, showing it, locking it or setting its opacity writes exactly one register, makes one commit, and the canvas is redrawn within 150 ms.
51. Given 10,000 objects, then moving one row to another place commits within 100 ms; moving 1,000 selected rows into another layer within 1 s; moving 10,000 within 5 s (with the wait cursor and the busy state of `0016` criterion 47a); a drag of many rows shows one count badge and no per-row preview.
52. Given a layer with 10,000 children, then expanding and collapsing it each take at most 100 ms.

## Decided by the product owner

Change any of these if you disagree. The ones with a cost for the customer are repeated as questions.

1. **The panel is a tab of the Properties panel, between Style and History** (Question 1). Reasons: the canvas keeps its 520 px at 800 x 600 (a left dock would take at least 200 px and leave under 320); `0043` was built for exactly this ("a tab is added by the spec that needs it"); the History tab already proved the pattern of a tab whose list fills the panel height and is the one scroll area; the key Shift+Ctrl+L is Inkscape's. The cost: Layers and Style cannot be seen side by side, so a maker who clicks a row and then wants to change its colour presses the Style tab (Shift+Ctrl+F) once. A selection made on the canvas switches Document to Style as before, but never leaves Layers.
2. **A layer is a group with a marker, at the top level only; layers are optional.** `0023` said "a layer is a top-level group that has a name and two flags"; that is kept. No sublayers (Inkscape and Illustrator have them; makers separate cut, engrave and reference with a flat set). A document may hold loose objects next to layers, so nothing changes for a file with no layers and no file is migrated or rewritten. New documents have no layer (as today).
3. **A layer is a container, not an object.** The canvas selection never holds a layer, so a group box, a style edit or Delete never targets a layer by accident. Clicking a layer row makes it the active layer; "Select contents" selects what is in it.
4. **Lock protects a node from being picked and edited itself; a container carries its locked members** (criterion 21).
5. **Hidden means not output.** Visibility is document state, so it is shared and part of the file; what is hidden is not exported and not produced (Question 2).
6. **Opacity is a node register on every node,** next to name, visible and locked, not part of the style. Rendered per paint (Question 5).
7. **Names, flags and opacity are shared document state** (last writer wins per register, ADR 0009 §3), not per-user view state, because the output depends on them. A per-user "hide for me" would be a separate flag later.
8. **Toggles are steps.** A hide or lock is a deliberate edit of the document and is undone with Ctrl+Z like any other.
9. **The list order is the stacking order, front-most at the top** (Inkscape, Illustrator and Affinity do the same).

## Out of scope

- **Sublayers**, layer colours, a target circle, duplicate layer, merge layers, "layers only" view, a layer's job assignment. The assignment of cut, engrave or reference to a layer is `0027`; this spec stores a name and three flags and nothing else on a layer.
- **Blend modes** and **group compositing** of opacity (Question 5). **Clip paths and masks.**
- **A separate Output flag** (LightBurn's Show and Output) and a per-user view-only hide (decision 7). Reference geometry that is visible but not cut is a role in `0027`.
- **Isolate (Alt+click on an eye), solo, "hide all others", lock all others.** Shift+click and Alt+click on the eye and the lock have no meaning in this slice beyond criterion 18.
- **Moving nodes to another layer by a command** (Inkscape's "Move to layer", Page Up and Down across layers). The drag and the Alt+Left and Alt+Right keys do it.
- **Searching and filtering the list**, by name, kind or flag.
- **Selecting by name or by flag** ("select all objects on this layer" is Select contents).
- **Several documents or pages** (dropped by the customer, 2026-10-05).
- **A left dock or a floating panel** (Question 1).
- **SVG import and export of layers** (`0024`). The mapping the SVG spec should use: a layer is `<g inkscape:groupmode="layer" inkscape:label="Cut">`, a hidden node `style="display:none"`, a locked node `sodipodi:insensitive="true"`, a name `inkscape:label`, opacity `opacity`. Because opacity here is multiplied per paint and SVG's is a group effect, the exported look can differ where fill and stroke or siblings overlap; the SVG spec says so.
- **Undo and redo** themselves (`0020`); this spec fixes the steps (criteria 44, 45).
- **Group transform, style and the other group rules** (`0023`).

## Open questions

Each has a default. Only the amendment of ADR 0002 §5 (`adrs.md`; also needed by `0049`) needs the customer's yes; Questions 1 and 2 are the ones he may care about.

1. **Where the panel lives.** *A (default, recommended):* a Layers tab of the Properties panel (decision 1). *B:* a second dock on the left of the canvas. It shows Layers and Style at once, but costs at least 200 px of canvas width (under 320 px of canvas at 800 x 600), which the 800 x 600 rule of the earlier specs cannot afford. *C:* a tab now, a dock for large windows later. Recommendation A.
2. **Hidden objects and the output (criterion 24).** *A (default):* a hidden node is not exported and not produced; the job preview (`0029`) says how many hidden objects are left out. *B:* hidden is a view setting only and everything is produced. *C:* an extra Output flag per layer (LightBurn), so "visible but not cut" is possible. Recommendation A: B can cut a part the maker hid on purpose; C is `0027`'s role "reference".
3. **Click on a layer row (criterion 13).** *A (default):* it activates the layer and clears the selection; Select contents is a row action. *B:* it selects the layer's contents at once, as selecting a group. B makes "I only want to draw in this layer" costly. Recommendation A.
4. **Locked members of a selected group (criterion 21).** *A (default):* the group's transform and style edit carry them. *B:* locked descendants are skipped, so a group can end up torn. Recommendation A.
5. **How opacity composes (criterion 27).** *A (default):* per paint, multiplied (what the renderer can do today). *B:* as SVG's group opacity: a node or layer is painted as a whole and then faded, so overlapping fill and stroke or overlapping siblings do not show through. B needs an off-screen pass in the renderer for every node with opacity below 100 %; the architect gives the cost. Recommendation A for this slice, B as its own entry if makers notice.
6. **Ctrl+A across layers (criterion 42).** *A (default):* all visible, unlocked objects of all layers (Illustrator); locking a layer is how a maker keeps Ctrl+A off it. *B:* only the active layer (Inkscape). Recommendation A.
7. **Layers optional, loose objects allowed (decision 2).** *A (default).* *B:* the first New layer wraps every loose object into "Layer 1" and a document always keeps at least one layer. B migrates, or surprises, every file the first time a layer is added. Recommendation A.
8. **Opacity editing (criterion 26).** *A (default):* the Opacity field above the list acts on the target, every row shows a muted percentage. *B:* a field inside each row (too wide for 244 px). *C:* a popover per row (Inkscape; the panel takes no popups).
9. **Canvas z-order keys (criterion 33, Proposal).** *A (default):* Home, End, Page Up, Page Down in the Select tool. *B:* the list only. Recommendation A; they are the commands `0016` named as a follow-up.
10. **An object moved into a layer that a peer deletes at the same moment.** In a shared document the object goes with the layer. *A (default):* accepted for now, as for groups (`0023` `adrs.md`, concurrency decision), and visible in the History tab, where `0042` can restore it. *B:* a repair step at merge that lifts such objects to the top level, as the orphan repair of `0049`. The architect says whether B can be done reliably (`adrs.md`); sharing does not exist yet.

## UX notes

Confirmed and completed by `ux-engineer`, 2026-10-10. The product owner's draft stands except where a line says "Changed". Numbers and rows are in `docs/design-system.md`: "Properties panel: Layers tab" and "Properties panel: tabs". References (`docs/reference-tools.md`): Inkscape's Layers and Objects dialog (tree, eye and lock per row, rename by double-click, drag to reorder), Illustrator and Affinity (Opacity above the list, lock and visibility per row), LightBurn (layers as a palette, no tree).

### Strip and layout

- **Strip. Changed: the subject line has 80 px, not 88.** Four tabs of 36 x 28 px with 4 px gaps are 156 px, and 8 px separate them from the subject line (`0043`), so 80 px are left. The subject line stays in the row (a row of its own would cost 24 px in every tab). It is cut with an ellipsis, its tooltip carries the full text, and no fact lives only there (the Style tab's clone block names the original in full, `0049`). Without History the strip has three tabs and the subject line 120 px. Icon Lucide `Layers`, 18 px, 1.5 px stroke; tooltip "Layers (Shift+Ctrl+L)".
- **Tab.** A column that fills the body: Opacity header (52 px: 28 px field row, 16 px caption, 8 px gap), the list, the footer (12 px padding, 28 px buttons). At 800 x 600 (panel viewport about 546 px, strip row 36 px) the list is about 418 px, 14 rows; the browser build, 36 px shorter, 13. The inline confirm of criterion 36 takes the footer's place and grows upward (at most 104 px), so the list may show 11 rows in the browser build while it is open; that state is transient and the footer never scrolls.
- **Header slot is reserved. Changed: nothing is targeted no longer removes the Opacity row.** The 52 px stay (the list must not jump when the selection empties); the field leaves the tree as criterion 26 says and the caption reads muted "Select an object or a layer." (target locked: "Locked").
- **Footer.** "New layer" (`Plus`) at the left, "Delete layer" (`Trash2`) at the right, 118 px each, 8 px apart. When Delete layer is not in the tree its 118 px slot stays empty (the Undo and Redo precedent), so New layer never moves. Notices: "Deleted layer Cut and 12 objects." and the locked-layer refusal go to the canvas notice slot, because the Delete key causes them too.

### Rows

- **Width budget** (rows are 244 px; the list reserves a stable 8 px scrollbar gutter, so a row is 236 px). Fixed left part at level 1: indent 16, chevron slot 16, 4, glyph 16, 4 = 56 px. Fixed right part: 4, eye 24, lock 24 = 52 px; the muted opacity readout (28 px, 12 px tabular, right-aligned) is added only when it shows. The name gets 128 px at level 1 (about 18 characters of 14 px text, so "Rectangle 3f9a1c" fits), 100 px with a readout, 16 px less per further level, 48 px at level 6. A layer (level 0) gets 144 px. A longer name is cut with an ellipsis and the tooltip shows it whole (a second muted line gives the state: "Hidden", "Locked", "Hidden by layer Cut").
- **Chevron** hit area 24 x 28 centred on its 16 px glyph; leaves keep the empty slot so glyphs line up. Eye and lock are 24 x 24 inside the 28 px row.
- **Row action** (criterion 6): a 24 px icon button over the end of the name, with the row's ground behind it, shown on hover, on focus within, on selected rows and always on the active layer's row, so a touch tap or a keyboard focus reaches it and it is never hover-only. Layer: `ListChecks` "Select contents". Clone: `Locate` "Select original" (`0049`). Leaf and group rows: none. It hides the last 24 px of a long name while shown; the tooltip has the full name, and nothing reflows.
- **Select contents with nothing to select** (the layer is empty, hidden or locked) changes nothing and posts "Layer Cut is empty." / "Layer Cut is hidden." / "Layer Cut is locked." to the canvas notice slot (`role="status"`, 3 s).
- **Selected, active, entered.** Selected row: the design system's List row look, `--value-fill` ground plus a 3 px `--accent` bar at the left edge (**Changed:** the draft said `--toolbar-icon-active-bg`, which is the tool button blue and would drown the glyphs). Active layer: the same 3 px bar and a semibold name, but **no ground** (a layer row can never be selected, so the bar on a layer means "active layer" and the ground on an object means "selected"); `aria-current="true"`. A group the maker entered (`0023`): a 1.5 px `--accent` outline inside its row and `aria-current="true"`, and while a group is entered the active layer's bar and `aria-current` are not drawn, because new objects go into the group (criterion 35); rows are not dimmed outside it (the canvas does that). Hover: `--editor-accent-hover`, which a selected row's ground overrides. Focus: 2 px `--editor-accent` ring inside the row.
- **Eye and lock. Changed: the calm glyph is `--lane-line` (3.1:1), not "40 %".** An unhidden, unlocked glyph at 40 % of `--toolbar-icon` is about 2:1 and these are working controls, not disabled ones. Calm state: `Eye` and `LockOpen` in `--lane-line`. Own hidden or locked: `EyeOff` and `Lock` in full `--toolbar-icon` (8.3:1), name in `--panel-muted-fg` for hidden. Inherited only: the same `EyeOff` or `Lock` in `--panel-muted-fg` inside a 1 px dotted ring; if the node has its own flag too, the own look wins. Hover and focus raise the calm glyph to `--toolbar-icon`. Shape, not colour, tells the states apart.
- **Collapsed container with a selected descendant:** the 6 px mark after its name is a filled diamond in `--accent` with the tooltip "Contains the selection"; the accessible name says "contains the selection".
- **Rename.** The name cell becomes a 28 px text field (white ground, 14 px, the field rules of the panel), arrow keys move the caret and not the rows, the first Escape cancels the edit and stays in the list, the second returns to the canvas. A press elsewhere commits (criterion 16).

### Opacity

The field is the Style tab's `ValueField` (spinbutton, "drag scrubs, click types", `0047` leaves it alone), label "Opacity" in the 60 px column, caption under it in 12 px `--panel-muted-fg` ("3 objects", "Layer Cut"; "Mixed" is shown in the field itself). While the target is a layer the caption starts with the `Layers` glyph (12 px), so the maker sees why Delete or Esc do not act on an object. The per-paint rule of criterion 27 is shown in the field's tooltip: "Fades fill and stroke separately. Overlapping parts show through."

### Drag

Pointer events, not HTML5 drag and drop (its drop events are unreliable in WebKitGTK and clash with the OS file drop). Threshold 4 px. The dragged rows show as a 70 % ghost at the pointer with a count badge for several; the insertion line is 2 px `--accent`, a container target is outlined 1.5 px `--accent`; a collapsed container under the pointer for 600 ms expands. Edge zone one row (28 px) tall at the top and bottom of the list scrolls; no easing, so nothing for reduced motion to change. Not allowed: `not-allowed` cursor and the reason as tooltip (criterion 30). Cursor `grabbing` while dragging. A drag started on the eye, the lock, the chevron or a text field never starts a row drag.

### Keyboard and screen reader

- One Tab stop; Tab order in the panel: collapse tab, strip, the Opacity field, the list, New layer, Delete layer. Roving focus as criterion 38; the footer buttons are two more stops.
- **Alt+arrows are in tension with browsers.** Alt+Left and Alt+Right are Back and Forward, Alt+Home is the home page, in Chrome, Edge and Firefox on Windows and Linux; the desktop windows use WebKitGTK, WebView2 and WKWebView. The build checks that `preventDefault` on keydown stops each on every platform (criterion 32). If one platform cannot, the keys on that platform become Shift+Alt+Left and Shift+Alt+Right (and the same with Home and End), the tooltip and the key table say so, and nothing else changes. Alt+Up and Alt+Down have no browser meaning.
- **Virtualised grid.** The grid carries `aria-rowcount` (all visible rows, collapsed ones excluded) and each row element `aria-rowindex`, besides `aria-level`, `aria-posinset`, `aria-setsize`; the focused row's element is kept in the page when it scrolls out of the 60-row window, so focus is never lost. Announcements (a reorder, "Deleted layer Cut and 12 objects.") use one visually hidden `role="status"` region of the panel; the list has no live region.
- Focus after Delete layer: the next row toward the back, or the previous one, or the New layer button.

### Touch and pointer type

The browser build on a tablet works; there is no mobile code (`CLAUDE.md` §6). With `pointer: coarse` the rows are 40 px and the chevron, eye and lock hit areas 36 x 40 px (the name loses 24 px of width), the footer buttons 36 px; a drag starts after a 400 ms hold on the glyph or name (a vertical pan scrolls, `touch-action: pan-y`); nothing needs hover (row actions: above). The 12-row rule of criterion 3 is for a fine pointer; a coarse pointer shows at least 9 at 800 x 600. A tablet without a keyboard has no reorder by key; the long-press drag is its route, and a "Move up / Move down" row action is a later entry if the customer wants one.

### While the Pen has an unfinished path

As the History list (`0043` criterion 10): the list scrolls, expands and collapses, but every press that would write or select (a row, an eye, a lock, a drag, a rename, New layer, Delete layer, the Opacity field) shows the hint "Finish the path (Enter) or cancel it (Esc) first." and changes nothing. A gap in the criteria: the product owner adds a criterion for it.

### Fit with the neighbours

- **`0043` tabs.** Layers is sticky like History: a selection change never makes it active and never leaves it (criteria 2, 10); Shift+Ctrl+F leaves it for Style or Document; Shift+Ctrl+D for Document. A click on a layer row clears the selection, so the Style tab goes dimmed (`0043` criterion 4) while Layers is shown; that is consistent and needs nothing.
- **`0044` Ctrl+A** with the Layers tab shown selects across layers (criterion 42); the rows follow in the same frame and the list expands only the first selected row's ancestors (criterion 12).
- **`0023` groups.** A click on a row inside another group enters it first, the canvas dims as for a double-click, and the group's row gets the outline above. Shift+click with the anchor row under another parent adds only the clicked row, like Ctrl+click (the range of criterion 11 exists only among siblings).
- **`0047` and `0019` transforms.** Nothing in this tab transforms; a transform of a selected group carries locked members (criterion 21), and the row shows no change. The typed move and the handles work as before, whichever tab is shown.
- **`0049` clones.** A clone row shows `Link2` as its kind glyph, the name "Clone of <origin name>" and the row action Select original; its flags and opacity are its own (criterion 25). Accessible name "Clone of Path 3f9a1c, linked clone, opacity 60 percent". An origin that is hidden or locked cannot be selected, so Select original answers with a notice (`0049` criterion 25).
- **Left rail (`0048`).** The tab adds nothing to the rail and the rail's cards are not touched; the Path and Boolean cards still work on the selection the list made. A layer row never selects, so with only a layer clicked the rail's commands dim as for an empty selection.
- **Canvas.** Nothing is added for layers. A hidden node is not there, a locked node is drawn and cannot be picked, the entered-group dimming and the layer opacity multiply (criterion 27).
